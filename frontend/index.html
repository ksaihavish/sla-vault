SLA-Vault — Blockchain Protocol Overview
SaaS providers lock collateral in an on-chain escrow vault as a performance bond. An independent oracle submits signed uptime telemetry. When a verified breach occurs, the smart contract automatically deducts the penalty from the locked collateral and transfers it directly to the customer — no support tickets, no manual disputes.
